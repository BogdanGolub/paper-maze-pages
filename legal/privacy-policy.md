# Privacy Policy for Paper Maze

**Last updated:** August 16, 2026

Paper Maze is a mobile multiplayer maze racing game. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and how to contact us.

If you have questions, contact us at **papermazeapp@gmail.com**.

## Information We Collect

Paper Maze collects only the information needed to run the game and support multiplayer matches.

### Account and Profile Information

Paper Maze may collect:

- A device-based player identifier used to sign in to the game.
- Your display name.
- Your game profile, including match count, wins, losses, best time, and recent match history.
- Language or app settings selected in the app.

### Multiplayer and Gameplay Information

When you play online, Paper Maze may process:

- Room codes and match identifiers.
- Lobby status, readiness status, and player connection status.
- Player color marker, position, movement commands, match timer, match result, and basic match history.
- Reconnect state needed to restore an interrupted match.

This information is used to run real-time multiplayer matches and show results to players in the same room.

### Technical Information

Paper Maze and its backend server may process technical information such as:

- Device/network connection metadata required to communicate with the game server.
- Server logs used to diagnose crashes, connection problems, abuse, or gameplay errors.
- Approximate timestamps of requests, matches, and connection events.

Paper Maze does not request precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or SMS access.

## How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

- Create and join multiplayer rooms.
- Run real-time maze races.
- Keep matches fair and server-authoritative.
- Restore matches after short connection interruptions.
- Save basic profile statistics and recent match history.
- Diagnose bugs, crashes, connectivity problems, and abuse.
- Provide support when you contact us.

## Sharing Information

Paper Maze does not sell personal information.

Paper Maze may share limited gameplay information with other players in the same match, such as your display name, player marker, ready status, connection status, position, and match result.

Paper Maze may use backend infrastructure providers to operate the game server and database. These providers process information only as needed to provide the game service.

We may disclose information if required by law, to protect the security of the service, or to investigate abuse.

## Advertising and Analytics

The current version of Paper Maze does not include third-party advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or in-app purchases.

If this changes, this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data Safety information will be updated before release.

## Data Retention

Paper Maze keeps account, profile, and match history information for as long as needed to provide the game service, maintain player stats, prevent abuse, and diagnose issues.

Server logs are kept only as long as reasonably needed for operations, debugging, and security.

You can permanently delete your guest account and its associated profile and gameplay data in the app under **Settings → Account → Delete player account**. If you no longer have access to the app, submit a request through the [Paper Maze account deletion page](../delete-account.html).

## Children

Paper Maze is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at **papermazeapp@gmail.com** and we will take appropriate action.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed by Paper Maze. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

## International Processing

Information may be processed on servers located outside your country or region. By using Paper Maze, you understand that information may be processed where the game backend is hosted.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, backend, legal requirements, or data practices change. The updated version will be posted with a new "Last updated" date.

## Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact:

**papermazeapp@gmail.com**
