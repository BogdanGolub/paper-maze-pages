A few notes before the starting line.

How does a race work?

How many friends can join?

Multiplayer rooms support 2–10 players. Everyone must be ready before the host can press Start match. Daily Maze is a solo timed challenge.

When does the multiplayer race end?

The first player to reach the goal wins and ends the race for the room. A race can also end when only one active player remains or the timer runs out. The result screen shows all players and your completed route.

Can a cosmetic make me faster?

No. Characters, trails, paper and effects change appearance only. They don’t add speed or a gameplay advantage.
